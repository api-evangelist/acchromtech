## What the company sells

- Liquid chromatography systems: HPLC and UHPLC
- Triple-quadrupole liquid chromatograph–mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS)
- Specialised instruments: PAHs analyser, semi-preparative liquid chromatography, online SPE
- Chromatographic columns and consumables: Alphasil, StarCore, SelectPrep, Marsil series
- Chromloong CDS — a self-developed chromatography data system for chromatographic information
  management, with distributed storage, standard network deployment and full-link audit

Markets served: pharmaceutical, food safety, environmental protection, disease control, clinical
testing, inspection and quarantine, petrochemical, and academic research laboratories.
